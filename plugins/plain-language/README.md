# plain-language

Makes the agent explain things in plain, jargon-free language to a smart reader who has no idea what it is talking about, without talking down to them, and lead with why it matters to the reader.

## Why

After hours inside a task, an agent writes for itself: terms it picked up along the way, labels it made up, and how things work instead of why they matter. The reader can't follow and can't act. The usual overcorrection, explaining it like to a five-year-old, is just as bad: it drops facts and patronises. This skill writes as if the reader has no idea what the agent is talking about, but is smart. That holds for every reader, even one who was there the whole time. Plain words, full substance, why it matters first. Because an agent cannot see the gaps in its own text, important texts also get read by a reviewer who has none of the agent's context.

## Usage

- Claude Code: `/plain-language`
- Codex: `$plain-language`
- Pi: `/skill:plain-language`

Or just say: "Explain it plainly." / "No jargon." / "Explain it like I have no idea."

Also self-invokes before any final message, explanation or document.
